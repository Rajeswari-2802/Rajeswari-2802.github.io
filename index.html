01 · About

Building hardware that listens, senses and reacts.

I'm Rajeswari, a B.E. Electronics and Communication Engineering student (2023–2027) focused on embedded systems and VLSI fundamentals.

My work sits where circuits meet code: reading sensors through ADCs, writing firmware for STM32, ESP32-S3, Arduino and Raspberry Pi, and shaping raw signals into decisions a machine or a driver can act on.

Industry training with NSIC in embedded AI/IoT and PLC-based industrial automation has shown me how these systems behave outside the lab, and I'm looking for a team where I can keep building them.
