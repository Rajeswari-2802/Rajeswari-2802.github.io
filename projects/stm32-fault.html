P/04 · STM32 · ADC

Machine fault monitoring on STM32.

A real-time monitoring system that samples a machine's sound and temperature through the STM32's on-chip ADC and flags abnormal operation, a bare-metal foundation for industrial condition monitoring.

TypeCondition monitoring
ControllerSTM32
InterfaceOn-chip ADC
IDESTM32CubeIDE
Fig. 1: Two analog sensors sampled by the STM32 ADC and classified as normal or fault.

The goal

Machines usually get noisier and hotter before they fail. This project captures both signals in real time on an industrial-grade microcontroller and turns them into a simple, immediate fault indication.

Why STM32

STM32 is widely used in industrial embedded products. Working at the ADC level with STM32CubeIDE builds skills that transfer directly: peripheral configuration, sampling and real-time firmware.

How it works

ConfigureADC channels and GPIO set up in STM32CubeIDE.
SampleSound and temperature sensors are read continuously through the ADC.
EvaluateFirmware compares readings against expected operating ranges.
IndicateReadings outside range raise a fault indication in real time.

Hardware

STM32ARM Cortex-M microcontroller
ADCOn-chip analog-to-digital converter for sensor input
Sound sensorCaptures machine noise level
Temperature sensorTracks machine heating

What it builds toward

  • Foundation for SUMA-TEX: the same sense-and-detect idea, later expanded to four modalities and ML.
  • Low-level control: direct peripheral work instead of relying on high-level libraries.
