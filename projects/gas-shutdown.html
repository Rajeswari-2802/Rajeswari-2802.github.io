Gas leak detection that acts, not just alarms.

An Arduino-based LPG leakage detector that doesn't stop at a warning: when gas crosses a safe threshold it sounds an alarm, shows the status on an LCD and turns a servo to close the gas line automatically.

TypeSafety / automation
ControllerArduino Uno
SensorMQ-6 LPG
ResultTop 5, Mini Project Competition
Fig. 1: MQ-6 reading drives three simultaneous responses once the threshold is crossed.

The problem

Most household gas alarms only beep. If nobody is home, or the person is asleep, the leak continues. The response that actually prevents an accident, closing the supply, still depends on a human.

The solution

Close the loop in hardware. The same controller that detects the leak also drives a servo coupled to the valve, so shutdown happens within the detection cycle, with an audible alarm and an on-screen status for anyone nearby.

How it works

MonitorThe MQ-6 sensor continuously reports LPG concentration to the Arduino's analog input.
DecideEmbedded C firmware compares readings against a safety threshold.
AlertThe buzzer sounds and the LCD switches from normal to a leak warning.
Shut downThe servo rotates to close the gas valve automatically.

Components
