SUMA-TEX: hearing a loom fail before it stops.

Smart Universal Maintenance Assistant for Textile MSMEs: a multimodal condition-monitoring system that watches power-looms through four kinds of sensors and flags developing faults, so small mills can plan maintenance instead of losing shifts to breakdowns.

TypePredictive maintenance
ControllerESP32-S3
Sensing4 modalities
StackPython · FastAPI · ML
Fig. 1: Four sensor streams fused on the ESP32-S3, analysed on a FastAPI backend and surfaced on a dashboard.

The problem

Textile MSMEs run power-looms for long hours with little monitoring. Faults such as bearing wear, misalignment or motor overload usually show up only when the machine stops, costing production time and repair money.

The approach

Every mechanical fault leaves a trace: a change in vibration, a new sound, extra heat or a shift in current draw. SUMA-TEX reads all four together, because one sensor alone is easily fooled by normal operating changes.

How it works

SenseVibration, acoustic, temperature and motor-current signals are captured from the loom.
ProcessThe ESP32-S3 acquires the signals and extracts features such as statistics and frequency content.
ClassifyA FastAPI backend runs an ML model that separates normal behaviour from abnormal patterns.
ActHealth status and alerts are shown on a dashboard for maintenance decisions.

Sensor suite

ESP32-S3Main controller: acquisition, processing, Wi-Fi
MPU6050Vibration via accelerometer / gyroscope
MAX9814Microphone amplifier with AGC for acoustic signature
DS18B20Digital temperature sensor on the machine body
SCT-013Non-invasive current transformer on the motor supply

Why multimodal

  • Cross-checking: a vibration spike paired with a current rise is far more telling than either alone.
  • Non-invasive: clamp-on and surface-mount sensors fit existing looms without modification.
  • Low cost: built from commodity parts so it suits small textile units, not just large mills.
